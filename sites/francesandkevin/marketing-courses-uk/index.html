Frequently asked

About our marketing courses

Is the Marketing Leadership Programme CPD accredited?
+
The Marketing Leadership Programme is CPD accredited. You receive a CPD certificate on completion which counts toward your annual CPD requirement.
Are the courses live or pre-recorded?
+
Most Frances and Kevin cohort programmes are delivered live online so you can ask questions and get direct feedback. Digital PR is currently self-paced. The format is stated on each individual course page before checkout or registration.
Who leads the courses?
+
James McCracken FCIM leads all programmes. He is a practising Fractional CMO, Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Marketing, and Chair of CIM North West. He has over 20 years of senior marketing leadership across education, property, SaaS and professional services. Every session is taught from lived experience, not from a textbook.
Can my employer fund the course?
+
Yes. CPD-accredited training is widely funded by employers as professional development. We can provide a CPD justification document and an invoice addressed to your employer. Payment plans are also available for all multi-week programmes.
How do I choose the right course?
+
The right course depends on where you are in your career. If you are a Senior Marketing Manager or Head of Marketing looking to step up, the Marketing Director Course is the right starting point. If you want to learn strategic marketing planning, SOSTAC Masterclasses. If you are considering going freelance or interim, From In-House, In-Demand. If you run a business and want to market it more effectively, Marketing for Business Owners. If you are not sure, talk to James first.
