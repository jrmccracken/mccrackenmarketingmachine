Why this programme

The AI Course Built for People Who Already Know Marketing

Most AI marketing courses are built for people who are new to marketing. They explain what a prompt is. They show you how to use ChatGPT to write a caption. That is not what this is. This programme is for experienced marketing managers and directors who already understand marketing strategy, and need a structured approach to making AI work at their level - not just in their own workflow, but across their team and in front of their leadership.

Is this for you?

You are ready for this if…

You are a marketing manager, senior marketing manager, head of marketing, or marketing director who wants a structured approach to AI

You are using AI tools already but you do not have a strategy for it and you are not sure what the right tools are or how to scale it

You need to talk about AI confidently with your leadership team and board, and you want a framework for doing that rather than talking generally about hype

You have a team and you need to upskill them in AI without losing quality, brand consistency, or control

You want practical development rather than a theory-heavy course that lists AI tools without showing you how to think about them strategically

You want to be the person in your organisation who leads on AI rather than the person who is still catching up in 12 months

Session 01
Where AI Actually Fits in a Marketing Function
How to audit your current workflows and identify where AI creates genuine value and where it simply creates noise. Which tools matter at a strategic level and which are tactical distractions. How to make a decision about AI investment without being distracted by hype.
Session 02
AI for Content, Campaigns, and Production
How senior marketers should brief, prompt, and quality-control AI-generated content. Maintaining brand voice and consistency at scale. Building production workflows that speed up output without introducing generic content or brand drift. Practical session using real tools.
Session 03
AI for Insight, Reporting, and Board Communication
Using AI for competitor analysis, customer research, and performance reporting. How to get board-level insights faster and present AI-assisted analysis with confidence. What to say when leadership asks what you are doing about AI - and how to say it without overpromising.
Session 04
Building an AI-Ready Marketing Team
How to upskill a team, set guardrails, and create governance that protects quality and brand. The output session: you leave with a finished 90-day AI integration roadmap for your own function, ready to present to your leadership team or act on straight away.

Common Questions

FAQs

Do I need technical knowledge or coding skills?
+
No. This programme is designed for marketing professionals, not developers. You need marketing experience, not technical ability. We do not cover how to build AI systems. We cover how to think about and use AI strategically as a senior marketer.
How is this different from free AI courses online?
+
Free courses are typically aimed at beginners and focus on what AI is and how to use basic tools. This programme is for experienced marketers who already understand marketing strategy. We focus on where AI fits in a senior marketing function, how to lead on it, and how to build a plan that works for your specific team and organisation.
What is the 90-day AI integration plan?
+
By the end of Session 4, you will have produced a structured plan covering which AI workflows you are implementing, how you are upskilling your team, what governance you are putting in place, and what you are measuring. It is designed to be presentable to your leadership team or board, not just notes from a course.
Can my organisation send multiple people?
+
Yes. If you want to send two or more people from the same team, contact James directly for a group rate. We also offer bespoke in-house versions of this programme for larger marketing teams who want it tailored to their specific tools and context.
When does the first cohort start?
+
The programme runs live online in a small cohort. Ask us about the next available dates.
Is this live online?
+
Yes. Like all Frances and Kevin programmes, this course is accredited by the CPD Certification Service. You will receive a completion certificate on completion which counts towards your professional development record.
