What's in the guide

Six sections covering the things most UK business owners get wrong with their marketing, and how to fix them.

01
Positioning before channels

Why starting with "which social media should I be on?" is the wrong question, and what to do first instead.

02
Choosing the right channels

How to pick two or three channels that match your business and customers, rather than spreading yourself thin across eight.

03
Your 12-month plan

A simple structure for writing a marketing plan that you will actually use, with four questions every plan needs to answer.

04
Setting a marketing budget

How UK businesses typically allocate marketing spend, and a simple framework for deciding what to invest at your stage of growth.

05
Capturing and nurturing leads

Why most marketing loses people at the wrong moment, and how to build a simple system that converts interest into customers.

06
Knowing when to get help

How to decide whether to hire an agency, a freelancer, or an in-house person, and what you need to know before you do.

You are running a small or medium business in the UK

And your marketing is inconsistent, reactive, or just not generating the enquiries it should.

🤔

You know you should be doing more with marketing

But you are not sure where to start, or your efforts so far have not produced the results you expected.

💸

You have spent money on marketing without a clear return

Ads, agencies, social media - and you are still not sure what is actually working or why.
