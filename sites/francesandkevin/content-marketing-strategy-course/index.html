Why this programme

Content needs a job, not just a calendar

This planned programme is for marketers who are producing plenty of content but need a clearer system for deciding what to create, who it is for, where it should work and how to judge whether it is earning its place.

Who it is for

Designed for working marketers

  • Marketing managers responsible for content strategy or editorial planning
  • B2B marketers trying to connect content with pipeline and sales
  • SEO, social and communications specialists moving into broader strategy roles
  • Teams producing lots of content without a clear measurement framework

What it would cover

01

02

Content pillars, formats and channel roles

03

04

05

Distribution, sales enablement and promotion

06

Content measurement, audits and improvement
