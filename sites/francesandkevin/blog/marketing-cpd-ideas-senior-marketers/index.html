Professional Development

Marketing CPD Ideas
for Senior Marketers

By James McCracken FCIM 20 June 2026 9 min read

Most senior marketers approach CPD as an obligation rather than an opportunity. You complete enough activities to hit your annual requirement, log them, write the minimum necessary reflections, and move on. The result is CPD that looks fine on paper and contributes very little to your actual development.

This guide is about doing it differently. Not because the CIM requires you to, but because the marketers who take CPD seriously tend to outperform those who treat it as a compliance exercise.

What Actually Counts as Marketing CPD

The CIM's CPD programme accepts a broad range of activities across four categories. Understanding these categories properly is the starting point, because most senior marketers significantly underestimate the range of activities that qualify.

Formal learning covers structured programmes, courses, qualifications, and accredited training. This includes programmes like the Frances and Kevin Marketing Leadership Programme, CIM courses, MBAs, and other formally delivered learning. Formal learning typically earns the highest credit value per hour.

Professional activity covers activities you carry out as part of your professional role that involve genuine development. Speaking at conferences, writing for professional publications, mentoring junior marketers, chairing committees, and contributing to industry bodies all count. James's role as Chair of CIM North West generates CPD credits, for example.

Practice-based learning covers learning that happens through the work itself. Stretching projects outside your normal scope, secondments, leading a function through significant change, or taking on responsibility for a part of the business you have not managed before. This category is particularly relevant for senior marketers who are already operating at a high level.

Self-directed learning covers reading, research, podcasts, webinars, and informal learning. This is the most accessible category but also the most limited in terms of credit value. The CIM caps self-directed learning at 25% of your total annual credits (8.75 credits out of 35). If most of your CPD is reading articles and listening to podcasts, you will struggle to hit the target through this route alone.

The Best CPD Activities for Senior Marketers in 2026

What counts as genuinely valuable CPD depends on where you are in your career. For senior marketers, the most valuable activities tend to be those that develop capabilities that are genuinely hard to build through day-to-day work alone.

Live, cohort-based learning programmes. The single most valuable CPD investment for most senior marketers is a structured live programme with expert input and peer discussion. The combination of expert facilitation, real-time feedback, and the perspectives of peers operating at a similar level is difficult to replicate through any other format. A seven-week programme like the Frances and Kevin Marketing Leadership Programme generates significant CPD credit and, more importantly, develops capabilities that are genuinely relevant to the director-level transition.

Speaking at events and conferences. Preparing and delivering a presentation on your area of expertise to a professional audience is one of the most underused CPD activities for senior marketers. It forces clarity of thought, builds professional profile, and counts toward your CPD. CIM regional events, marketing conferences, and industry forums all provide opportunities.

Mentoring junior marketers. Being a mentor or coach to someone earlier in their career generates CPD credits and, in practice, often teaches you as much as it teaches the mentee. Explaining what you know to someone who does not yet know it forces you to examine the reasoning behind your instincts, which is genuinely developmental.

Structured reading with reflection. The key word is structured. Reading a marketing blog post while eating lunch is not CPD. Reading a well-chosen book on a topic you want to develop, taking notes, and writing a short reflection on how the ideas apply to your current role is CPD. The difference is intentionality and application, not the medium.

Cross-functional projects. Senior marketers who take on responsibility for projects outside their normal remit, working closely with finance, operations, product, or sales, generate practice-based CPD credits while developing exactly the commercial fluency that director-level roles require.

Board or committee membership. Joining a board, committee, or advisory group, whether in your organisation or externally, develops governance, stakeholder management, and commercial decision-making skills that are directly relevant to senior marketing leadership. Many professional associations, charities, and local business networks actively seek marketing expertise on their governing bodies.

CPD Activities That Are Less Useful Than They Look

Not all CPD is equally valuable, and being selective matters more at senior level than at junior level. The following activities tend to generate more CPD hours than genuine development:

Webinars you attend but do not engage with. Sitting through a vendor webinar while checking your email counts as self-directed learning on paper but generates very little actual development. If you would not pay to attend it, it probably belongs in the self-directed learning category at the lowest credit rate.

Conferences where you attend every talk but make no connections. The networking and peer conversation at a good conference is often more valuable than the content on stage. If you attend but do not engage, you are leaving most of the value on the table.

Generic online courses that do not connect to a specific development need. Completing a LinkedIn Learning course on a topic you already understand well because it takes 45 minutes and generates a certificate is not serious CPD. The question to ask before any CPD activity is: what specific capability gap does this address, and how will I apply it?

How to Make Your CPD Count Toward Chartered Marketer

If you are working toward Chartered Marketer status, your CPD needs to be structured around the CIM's Global Professional Marketing Framework rather than simply accumulated. This means being deliberate about which competency areas you are developing each year and ensuring your reflective statements connect your learning activities to specific professional behaviours from the framework.

The CIM requires MCIM and FCIM members to complete four reflective statements per year for two consecutive years. Each statement should describe what you did, what you learned, and how it changed your practice. The quality of these statements matters, and "I attended a conference and found it useful" is not a reflective statement that will support a Chartered Marketer application.

The Frances and Kevin Marketing Leadership Programme is designed to generate substantive CPD content that directly supports Chartered Marketer applications. If you are an MCIM or FCIM working toward chartership, the programme provides structured learning that maps to the framework and generates the kind of applied development that produces strong reflective statements.

Planning Your CPD Year

The most effective approach is to plan your CPD at the start of the year rather than accumulating activities reactively and counting them up in September. Start with your development goals: what specific capabilities do you want to build or strengthen in the next 12 months? Then plan one or two significant formal learning activities to address the most important gaps, supplemented by professional activities and self-directed learning across the year.

A simple structure that works for most senior marketers: one formal programme or qualification (generating 15 to 20 credits), two or three professional activities such as speaking, mentoring, or committee work (generating 10 to 12 credits), and regular self-directed learning up to the 25% cap (8.75 credits). That gets you to 35 credits with activities that are genuinely developmental rather than simply compliant.

Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as CPD for marketers?
+
CPD for marketers falls into four categories recognised by the CIM: formal learning such as accredited courses and qualifications, professional activity such as speaking at events and mentoring, practice-based learning from stretching projects, and self-directed learning such as reading and webinars. Self-directed learning is capped at 25% of annual CPD credits.
How many CPD hours do marketers need?
+
CIM members need 35 CPD credits per year for ACIM grade. MCIM and FCIM members need four reflective statements per year rather than a credit target. The reflective statements must cover different competency areas across a two-year period for Chartered Marketer applications.
What are the best CPD activities for senior marketers?
+
The most valuable CPD for senior marketers is live cohort-based learning that develops commercial and leadership capability. Speaking at professional events, mentoring junior marketers, cross-functional projects, and board or committee membership are also highly effective.
