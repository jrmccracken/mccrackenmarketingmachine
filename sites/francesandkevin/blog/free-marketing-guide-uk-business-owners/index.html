A Free Marketing Guide for UK Business Owners

By James McCracken FCIM - Fellow, Chartered Institute of Marketing

Most small business marketing in the UK follows the same pattern. You try a few things, some of them work occasionally, most of them do not, and after a while the activity quietly stops because nothing has compounded into a system. The problem is rarely budget or effort. It is almost always the absence of a plan.

We have put together a free guide to help UK business owners fix that. It covers the things most businesses get wrong, the decisions that actually matter, and how to build a simple marketing approach that generates consistent results rather than occasional ones.

You can download it here for free - no sales pitch, no catch. Just your name and email, and we will send it straight to you.

What's in the guide

The guide covers six areas. All of them are things I see small and medium businesses in the UK struggle with regularly.

Positioning before channels

The most common question I get from business owners is "which social media should I be on?" It is the wrong starting point. Before you pick a channel, you need to know who your best customers are, what specific problem you solve for them, and why they would choose you over a competitor. When you can answer all three specifically, every channel you use becomes significantly more effective. The guide covers how to work this out without overcomplicating it.

Choosing the right channels

Most businesses try to be everywhere at once and end up being mediocre on eight channels rather than excellent on two. The guide helps you choose two or three channels that match your customers and your stage of growth, with practical guidance on what tends to work for different types of UK business.

Writing a 12-month plan

A plan does not need to be complicated. It needs to answer four questions: what you are trying to achieve commercially, which activities you are going to focus on, what the budget is, and how you will know if it is working. The guide walks through a simple structure that gives you those answers in a format you will actually use.

Setting a marketing budget

One of the most common questions at our Marketing for Business Owners programme is how much to spend. There is no universal answer, but there are sensible frameworks. The guide covers how UK businesses typically approach this and how to think about allocation at different stages of growth.

Capturing and nurturing leads

Most marketing drives people to a website or social profile and then loses them. They look, they do not buy immediately, and they never return. The guide covers how to build a simple lead nurturing approach that keeps you visible until the moment someone is ready to act.

When to get help

There is a point where the owner's time is better spent running the business than doing the marketing. The guide covers how to think about whether you need an agency, a freelancer, or an in-house hire, and what you need to understand about your own marketing before any of those conversations will go well.
