What the playbook form now does
-------------------------------
1. Captures every playbook request in Netlify Forms.
2. Unlocks the PDF immediately for every visitor.
3. If the visitor opts in to email, the Netlify Function adds/updates them in Mailchimp.
4. The function applies the Mailchimp tag: Strategic Playbook.
5. Your Mailchimp Automation Flow can trigger when that tag is added.

ONE SECRET YOU MUST ADD IN NETLIFY
----------------------------------
Do NOT put the API key in the website files.

In Netlify, open the Frances & Kevin project and add this environment variable:

Key: MAILCHIMP_API_KEY
Value: your Mailchimp API key

Make sure its scope includes Functions, then redeploy v17.

Mailchimp automation
--------------------
Create an Automation Flow / Customer Journey for the Frances & Kevin audience:
Trigger: Tag added -> Strategic Playbook
First action: Send email immediately
Suggested subject: Your Marketing Manager to Marketing Director Playbook
PDF URL: https://francesandkevin.co.uk/assets/marketing-manager-to-marketing-director-playbook.pdf

Important consent behaviour
---------------------------
The PDF unlocks whether or not the visitor opts into email marketing.
Only visitors who tick the email opt-in are synced as new subscribed contacts in Mailchimp.
This prevents the lead magnet itself from silently subscribing people to marketing.

Existing unsubscribed Mailchimp contacts are not forcibly resubscribed by the integration.
